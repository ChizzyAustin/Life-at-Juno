How it comes together

Five stages, one tally

Each stage hands the next one what it needs. The sign-ups give us squads and sizes, the vote gives us the names on the jerseys, and every competition after that feeds the points tally.

  1. 110 to 12 Nov

    Sign up and vote

    Members pick their games at life.juno.africa/derby and vote for their house name at life.juno.africa/names. Votes stay private until Friday.

    Gives us: players, jersey sizes, house names
  2. 2Fri 13 Nov

    Lock it in

    House names announced. Jerseys ordered with the new names. Brackets drawn and posted in both groups.

    Gives us: kit on order, fixtures out
  3. 314 to 19 Nov

    The build-up

    Squad training, then tennis, snooker, chess, board games and FC 27 run across both buildings.

    Every final scores
  4. 4Fri 20 Nov

    Derby Eve

    Kit collection at Karimu, the Wizard of Juno quiz and the FC 27 final on the big screen.

    Quiz and FC 27 score
  5. 5Sat 21 Nov

    Derby Day

    Side games and the chess final while everyone arrives, then the walk-out in the new names, the match and the final tally.

    The biggest points of the series

Every point goes into one tally.The Juno Derby Cup goes to the building with the most points after the last side game on Derby Day.

Derby Day on the pitch

Five hours, no dead time

Times count from when we get the pitch. The side games open the day while everyone arrives and give every member, not only the squads, a way to score. The match comes last, about two hours in, when the crowd is at its biggest.

How the tally adds up

Points go to the player's building, so a final between two players from the same building earns that building both the winner's and the runner-up's points.

24points in the build-up
19points on Derby Day
43points in total. Winning the match alone doesn't win the Cup.
