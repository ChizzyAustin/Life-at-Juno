HOUSE RULES AT JUNO

Here are important house rules you need to be aware of:

🚭Juno is a strictly non-smoking premises. Any violation of this rule will result in strict enforcement actions, including possible immediate termination of the License without refund.
🚫The use of Indian hemp, narcotics, or banned substances is strictly prohibited and may result in immediate termination of membership and forfeiture of any unused rent.
⏱️Membership Fee payments and renewals must be made on or before the due date to avoid late-payment penalty or membership cancellation.
🗓️Members planning to move out must provide at least two (2) weeks' written notice via email or WhatsApp. Failure to do so may result in forfeiture of the caution deposit.
🛡️The main access door will be closed and security guards will be on midnight patrols from 12:00am to 5:00am. Members requiring access during this period should contact the Security Team via the designated security phone number.
🪑Juno assets (including chairs and furniture) in the shared spaces must not be moved into rooms or removed from their designated areas. Members will be charged for any damage to Juno property.
🗑️Put all waste in the designated bins. Do not leave rubbish in hallways, rooms, staircases, or other common areas.
🛁Members must follow the bathroom-use instructions posted on the bathroom doors. This will help prevent pipe blockages and damage to plumbing facilities.
⛑️Promptly report any maintenance or safety concerns and cooperate with the Facilities Team during maintenance and repair activities.
EMERGENCY RESPONSE PLAN & SAFETY

SAFETY FIRST: What to do in a Fire Emergency

(When the fire alarm goes off)

1.STOP: Stop what you are doing and remain calm.
2.EXIT: Leave the building immediately through the nearest safe emergency exit.
3.HELP: If it is safe to do so, assist anyone who may need help.
4.ASSEMBLE: Proceed directly to the designated assembly point.
5.STAY: Remain at the assembly point and do not re-enter the building until you have been given the all-clear by the appropriate personnel.
Remember, never stop to collect personal belongings.
Your safety comes first.
