Juno at Five is this Friday, 6pm to 10pm on the Karimu rooftop. Here are three things to know before then.

THE ROOFTOP

Reserved for Juno at Five on Friday

Juno at Five is on the Karimu rooftop. On Friday 6 November the rooftop will be closed for general use, as it will be set up for the celebration and used until 10pm.

ENTRY

Strictly by registration

Access to the rooftop on Friday is strictly by registration, and only registered members will be let in. If you haven't registered yet, please RSVP by Thursday 5 November.
