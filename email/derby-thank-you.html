THE JUNO DERBY · THANK YOU

Hi Juno fam,

Thank you for making the first Juno Derby one to remember. From the first table tennis serve on 14 November to the final whistle at ISL on Saturday, both houses showed up for each other.

Saturday also gave both buildings their new names. Karimu Ikotun is now Juno Beacon: live bright, dream bigger. Emily Akinola is now Juno Atlas: the world is yours.

Congratulations to Juno Beacon, winners of the Juno Derby Cup, and to every player who took home a medal. Juno Atlas, the rematch starts now. Photos and videos from the week are on the way.
